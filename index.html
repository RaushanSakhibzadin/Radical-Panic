Jobs · what each friend does

Sprout shoots the nearest radical, steadily. Grower makes Nectar three times as fast but barely fights. Bulwark has triple the health and holds the line. Frost chills radicals so they crawl. Lobber arcs over the front rank at the furthest radical and splashes.

A friend's job is carried by a gene, so it is inherited and it mutates: keep planting Lobbers and you will be offered more of them, and now and then one of their children will come out as something else.

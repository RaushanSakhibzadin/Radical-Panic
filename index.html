How to play

  1. Kangxi radicals — 火 水 刀 鬼 龍 — march left across the field toward your bowls 🥣.
  2. Pick a card from your hand (click, or keys 1–4), then click a square to plant it. It costs 氣.
  3. Planted emoji shoot down the lane on their own. Lose three bowls and it is over.
  4. 氣 regenerates by itself and every radical you fell pays a bounty.

Why it keeps changing

Every card is an individual, not a type. It carries a genome: hp, attack, defence, speed and range, plus how it bobs, sways and squashes, plus the size of its eyes, the angle of its brows, how wide it smiles and how often it blinks. Each card is its parent's genome with a small random mutation, so no two are the same.

You are the selection pressure. Planting a card tells the game that lineage is worth keeping, and its mutations get passed on and offered more often. Composting one (🗑, or X) says the opposite, and so does leaving a card unplayed for a whole wave. Fitness fades a little every wave, so yesterday's favourite must keep earning it.

None of the emoji have eyes of their own — every face you see is drawn from that genome at runtime. Over an evening the roster drifts toward whatever you personally find cute, funny or effective, and it stays that way: the pool is saved in your browser and you can export it and trade it.

Privacy: everything is local. No server, no account, no analytics.
